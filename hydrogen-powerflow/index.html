02

What hydrogen changes in the power flow problem制氢给潮流问题带来了什么变化

The network equations do not change. Ybus, the mismatch equations and Newton–Raphson are the same as in any power flow. Each plant, battery and hydrogen plant is one more injection at its bus:

网络方程不变。Ybus、功率不平衡方程和牛顿–拉夫逊法与普通潮流计算完全相同。每座电站、每个电池储能和每个制氢站都只是在接入节点增加一项注入功率：

\[ \begin{aligned} P_i^{\text{sch}} &= P_{Gi} + P_{Wi} + P_{Si} + P_{Bi} + P_{Hi} - P_{Di}\\ Q_i^{\text{sch}} &= Q_{Gi} + Q_{Wi} + Q_{Si} + Q_{Bi} + Q_{Hi} - Q_{Di} \end{aligned} \]

Here PH is the hydrogen plant's net power: PH = Pfc − Pel − Psb, negative while the electrolyser runs and positive while a fuel cell feeds the grid. Psb is the small hot-standby load drawn while the stack is not producing. What makes a hydrogen plant different from a battery:

式中 PH 为制氢站的净功率：PH = Pfc − Pel − Psb。电解槽运行时为负，燃料电池向电网送电时为正；Psb 是电解槽不制氢时为保持热备用而消耗的少量功率。制氢站与电池储能的不同之处在于：

  • It is mostly a load. Most of the hydrogen leaves the site for industry or transport, so the plant draws far more energy than it returns. A fuel cell, if there is one, is usually much smaller than the electrolyser.
  • It cannot run at any power. Below a minimum load, typically 10–40% for alkaline and 0–10% for PEM electrolysers, the stack has to stop, so the power it can draw has a gap between zero and Pmin.
  • It stores a lot, but loses a lot. A tank holds days of output cheaply, but turning electricity into hydrogen and back returns only about a third of it, against roughly 88% for a lithium-ion battery.
  • Its rectifier may not control Q. Many large electrolysers use thyristor rectifiers, which draw reactive power at a lagging power factor. Section 05 shows how this pulls the bus voltage down.
  • 它主要是负荷。大部分氢气要外送给工业或交通用户，所以制氢站从电网吸收的电量远多于送回的电量。即使配有燃料电池，其功率通常也远小于电解槽。
  • 不能在任意功率下运行。低于最小负荷（碱性电解槽一般为 10%–40%，PEM 为 0–10%）时必须停止制氢，因此它可吸收的功率在 0 和 Pmin 之间有一段空缺。
  • 储得多，但损失也大。储氢罐可以低成本地储存数天的产量，但电转氢再转回电只能送回约三分之一，而锂离子电池约为 88%。
  • 整流器不一定能控制无功。许多大型电解槽采用晶闸管整流器，以滞后功率因数吸收无功。第 05 节会看到它如何拉低节点电压。

A hybrid storage station puts the two together. The battery is efficient and fast but holds only a few hours; the hydrogen plant is slow and lossy but can absorb surplus for days. At the same bus they can share one goal, such as firming a wind farm's output. The Lab setting When storage and hydrogen share a goal decides which acts first. The slack generator at bus 1 still balances whatever is left.

混合储能电站把两者结合起来。电池储能效率高、响应快，但只能储存几个小时；制氢站响应慢、损耗大，却能连续多天吸收富余电量。接在同一节点时，它们可以共同完成一个目标，比如平抑风电场的出力。实验台中的“储能与制氢目标相同时”决定谁先动作。剩下的不平衡功率仍由节点 1 的平衡机承担。

A solar plant's DC power is proportional to irradiance, falls as the cells heat up, and is clipped at the inverter rating. A battery's state of charge changes with the power it holds, with charge and discharge efficiencies ηc = ηd = √ηrt:

这些模型与风电光伏接入页面和储能接入页面相同，那里有完整的讲解。风电场在切入风速和额定风速之间沿三次曲线出力；光伏电站的直流功率与辐照度成正比，随电池温度升高而下降，并受逆变器容量限幅；电池储能的 SOC 随充放电功率变化，充、放电效率取 ηc = ηd = √ηrt：

04

The hydrogen plant: electrolyser, tank and fuel cell制氢站：电解槽、储氢罐与燃料电池

An electrolyser splits water into hydrogen and oxygen with direct current from a rectifier. Its specific energy consumption eel (kWh per kg of H₂) says how much electricity each kilogram takes. Complete systems today need about 50–55 kWh/kg. Hydrogen's lower heating value is 33.3 kWh/kg, so the electrolyser efficiency is ηel = 33.3/eel, around 60–67%. Drawing Pel MW produces hydrogen at

电解槽利用整流器提供的直流电把水分解为氢气和氧气。单位电耗 eel（kWh/kg H₂）表示生产每千克氢气所需的电量，目前完整的电解系统约为 50–55 kWh/kg。氢气的低位热值为 33.3 kWh/kg，因此电解效率 ηel = 33.3/eel，约为 60%–67%。电解功率为 Pel MW 时，产氢速率为

\[ \dot m_{\text{el}} = \frac{1000\,P_{\text{el}}}{e_{\text{el}}}\ \ \text{kg/h},\qquad P_{\min}\le P_{\text{el}}\le P_{\text{el,rated}} \]

The hydrogen goes to a tank of capacity M. An offtake D (kg/h) leaves continuously for industry or transport, and an optional fuel cell turns hydrogen back into electricity at efficiency ηfc, typically around 50%. The tank links one hour to the next, just as the SOC does for a battery:

氢气进入容量为 M 的储氢罐。外送 D（kg/h）持续供给工业或交通用户；可选的燃料电池以效率 ηfc（一般约 50%）把氢气重新转换为电能。储氢罐把前后各小时联系起来，作用与电池的 SOC 相同：

\[ \begin{aligned} m(t+\Delta t) &= m(t) + \left(\dot m_{\text{el}} - \dot m_{\text{fc}} - D\right)\Delta t,\qquad 0\le m\le M\\[4pt] P_{\text{fc}} &= \frac{\dot m_{\text{fc}}\cdot 33.3\,\eta_{\text{fc}}}{1000}\ \ \text{MW} \end{aligned} \]

Going from electricity to hydrogen and back returns ηelηfc ≈ 0.63 × 0.5 ≈ 31%. That is why hydrogen plants are built mainly to supply hydrogen, with power back to the grid as an option. As for the battery, a snapshot only allows what the plant can hold for one hour: the electrolyser may fill the tank only to the room left after the offtake, and the fuel cell may only use what is left after it. A request below Pmin sends the electrolyser to hot standby.

电转氢再转回电，只能送回 ηelηfc ≈ 0.63 × 0.5 ≈ 31%。因此建设制氢站主要是为了供氢，向电网送电只是附加功能。与电池储能一样，单张快照只允许制氢站按能维持一小时的功率运行：电解槽只能把扣除外送后剩余的罐容充满，燃料电池也只能使用扣除外送后剩下的氢气。要求的电解功率低于 Pmin 时，电解槽转入热备用。

The three dispatch rules apply to batteries and hydrogen plants alike, and are computed before the network is solved:

三种控制策略对电池储能和制氢站同样适用，都在求解网络之前算出：

05

Reactive power: converters and rectifiers无功功率：变流器与整流器

Wind turbines, solar inverters, batteries and electrolysers with an active front end all connect through IGBT converters, so the controller sets their reactive power, and the converter's apparent power rating S limits it. Active power takes its share first:

风机、光伏逆变器、电池储能和采用有源前端的电解槽都通过 IGBT 变流器并网，无功由控制器设定，并受变流器视在功率容量 S 的限制。有功优先：

\[ |Q| \le Q_{\text{cap}}=\sqrt{S^2-P^2} \]

A battery and a hydrogen plant both use all four quadrants of the circle. A thyristor rectifier is different. It controls the DC current by delaying the firing of each thyristor, and that delay makes the AC current lag the voltage. The rectifier therefore draws reactive power in proportion to the electrolyser power, and the controller cannot change it:

电池储能和制氢站都可以在圆的四个象限运行。晶闸管整流器则不同：它通过推迟每个晶闸管的触发来控制直流电流，这种延迟使交流电流滞后于电压。因此整流器吸收的无功与电解功率成正比，控制器无法改变：

\[ Q_H = -P_{\text{el}}\tan\varphi,\qquad \cos\varphi \approx 0.85\text{–}0.95 \]

For the power flow, the thyristor plant is simply a PQ load with fixed power factor. At a weak bus such as 14, 30 MW at pf 0.9 draws about 15 MVAr and can pull the voltage down by several percent. Real plants add filters and reactive compensation for this.

在潮流计算中，晶闸管整流的制氢站就是一个恒功率因数的 PQ 负荷。在 14 号这样的薄弱节点，30 MW、功率因数 0.9 的电解槽约吸收 15 MVAr，可使电压下降几个百分点。实际工程会为此配置滤波器和无功补偿装置。

采用 Q(V) 下垂控制时，给定无功取决于未知的电压：Q(|V|) = S(Vref − |V|)/D，并限制在 ±Qcap 以内。它的导数进入雅可比矩阵的 L 块，牛顿法因此仍保持快速收敛：

\[ L_{ii}=\frac{Q_i}{|V_i|}-B_{ii}|V_i|+\frac{S}{D} \]
06

How much should the electrolyser draw?电解槽该吸收多大功率？

An electrolyser changes the flows like any load. Next to a wind farm whose export overloads a line, it turns part of the surplus into hydrogen before it enters the network. At a load centre at peak time, it adds to the burden on the lines that feed it. The voltage changes as well, by roughly ΔV ≈ (R PH + X QH)/|V|.

电解槽改变潮流的方式与任何负荷相同。如果风电场送出功率使某条线路过载，在同一节点电解就能在多余功率进入电网之前把它转化为氢气；在负荷中心的高峰时段电解，则会加重向其供电的线路负担。电压也随之变化，约为 ΔV ≈ (R PH + X QH)/|V|。

07

Do hydrogen and storage raise the hosting capacity?制氢和储能能提高新能源承载能力吗？

Hosting capacity is how much renewable generation a network can take before a limit is broken. An electrolyser or battery at the right bus can absorb the part that would overload a line or push a voltage too high. The battery has to give it back later; the electrolyser keeps it as hydrogen.

新能源承载能力是指电网在不越限的前提下能接纳多少新能源出力。接在合适节点的电解槽或电池储能，可以吸收会导致线路过载或电压过高的那部分功率。电池之后还要把电量放回电网，电解槽则把它留在氢气里。

This sweep scales the installed wind and solar capacity in the Lab together, from 0 to 300%, twice: once with the storage and hydrogen plants as set in the Lab, and once without them. Units in the Setpoint or Level modes do not respond to local output; in the Firm mode they absorb everything above their target, until their ratings, the minimum load or the tank stop them.

下面的扫描把实验台中风电和光伏的装机容量同步从 0 放大到 300%，分别在“按实验台设置接入储能和制氢站”和“去掉它们”两种情况下各算一遍。给定功率和削峰填谷模式的设备不响应本地出力；平抑模式的设备会吸收超过目标值的全部出力，直到受额定功率、最小负荷或储氢罐限制。

What this model leaves out. Each plant, battery and hydrogen plant is one equivalent unit connected directly to its bus, without a collector network or step-up transformer. Electrolyser and fuel-cell efficiencies are constant: real stacks are more efficient at part load, while auxiliaries such as compressors, pumps and cooling cost relatively more. There is no start-up time, ramp limit, stack degradation or cold start, and compression energy for the tank is folded into the specific consumption. The thyristor rectifier has a constant power factor and no harmonics or filters. The dispatch rules are simple rules computed before the network solve, not an optimal power flow, and they ignore network losses and hydrogen prices. The page covers steady-state balanced operation only. It is for teaching, not for planning studies.

本模型未考虑的内容。每座电站、每个电池储能和每个制氢站都等值为一台直接接在节点上的设备，没有集电网络和升压变压器。电解槽和燃料电池效率为常数：实际电堆在部分负荷下效率更高，而压缩机、泵和冷却等辅助设备的相对能耗则更大。不考虑启动时间、爬坡限制、电堆衰减和冷启动，储氢压缩能耗计入单位电耗。晶闸管整流器的功率因数为常数，不考虑谐波和滤波器。控制策略是在求解网络之前算出的简单规则，不是最优潮流，不计网损，也不考虑氢气价格。本页只涉及三相平衡稳态运行，仅用于教学，不能用于规划研究。

Data. IEEE 14-bus test case from the University of Washington Power Systems Test Case Archive, with the same values as MATPOWER's case14.
