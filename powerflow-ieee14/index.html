01

What a power flow study answers潮流计算要回答什么问题

Every bus in a transmission network has four electrical quantities: real power injection P, reactive power injection Q, voltage magnitude |V| and voltage angle θ. Operators know some of them: how much each load draws and how much each generator is scheduled to produce. Power flow (also called load flow) finds the rest. The answer is the steady-state operating point of the whole network.

输电网中每个节点都有四个电气量：有功注入 P、无功注入 Q、电压幅值 |V| 和电压相角 θ。调度人员只知道其中一部分：每个负荷消耗多少功率、每台发电机计划发多少电。潮流计算（load flow）求出其余未知量，得到整个电网的稳态运行点。

Once every bus voltage is known, everything else follows from circuit laws: the power on each line and transformer, the reactive output each generator must supply, and the total losses. That is why power flow sits underneath almost every other study. Planning new lines, N−1 contingency screening, optimal power flow and state estimation all solve it, many times over.

一旦求出所有节点电压，其余结果都可以由电路定律直接算出：每条线路和变压器上的功率、每台发电机需要提供的无功、以及全网损耗。因此潮流计算几乎是所有其他分析的基础：电网规划、N−1 预想事故扫描、最优潮流和状态估计都需要反复求解潮流。

You give it已知输入
  • Load PD, QD at every bus
  • Generator real power PG and voltage setpoint
  • Network topology and branch parameters r, x, b, tap t
  • 各节点负荷 PD、QD
  • 发电机有功出力 PG 和电压设定值
  • 网络拓扑和支路参数 r、x、b、变比 t
It gives you计算输出
  • |V| and θ at every bus
  • Real and reactive flow on every branch
  • Reactive output of every generator, real output of the slack
  • Total system losses
  • 各节点电压幅值 |V| 和相角 θ
  • 各支路的有功和无功潮流
  • 各发电机的无功出力，以及平衡机的有功出力
  • 全网总损耗

Standard assumptions: balanced three-phase operation, represented by its positive-sequence single-phase equivalent; sinusoidal steady state at nominal frequency; all quantities in per unit on a 100 MVA base.

标准假设：三相平衡运行，用正序单相等值电路表示；额定频率下的正弦稳态；所有量都采用以 100 MVA 为基准的标幺值。

Three kinds of bus三类节点

Each bus contributes two equations, real and reactive power balance, so exactly two of its four quantities must be specified in advance. Which two depends on what is connected to it.

每个节点提供两个方程（有功平衡和无功平衡），因此四个量中必须预先给定两个。给定哪两个，取决于节点上连接了什么设备。

03

From one-line diagram to Ybus从单线图到节点导纳矩阵 Ybus

Each line is modelled as a π-equivalent: a series impedance z = r + jx between its two ends, and half of the total line-charging susceptance b to ground at each end. A tap-changing transformer adds an ideal transformer with ratio t : 1 on its from side. IEEE 14 has three of them, on branches 4–7, 4–9 and 5–6.

每条线路用 π 型等值电路表示：两端之间是串联阻抗 z = r + jx，两端各有一半线路充电电纳 b/2 接地。带分接头的变压器在首端串入一个变比为 t : 1 的理想变压器。IEEE 14 节点系统中有三台，位于支路 4–7、4–9 和 5–6。

Both models reduce to a 2 × 2 admittance block that relates the currents injected at the branch's two ends to the end voltages (for a plain line, t = 1):

两种模型都可以写成一个 2 × 2 导纳块，把支路两端注入电流与两端电压联系起来（普通线路取 t = 1）：

\[ y_s=\frac{1}{r+jx},\qquad \begin{bmatrix} I_f\\ I_t\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\dfrac{y_s+j\,b/2}{t^2} & -\dfrac{y_s}{t}\\[8pt] -\dfrac{y_s}{t} & y_s+j\,b/2\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}V_f\\ V_t\end{bmatrix} \]

Adding every branch's four entries into a 14 × 14 matrix gives the bus admittance matrix, which links injected currents to bus voltages: I = YbusV. Each diagonal entry is the sum of all admittances connected to that bus. Each off-diagonal entry is minus the admittance between two buses, and zero if they are not connected. The capacitor bank at bus 9 (19 MVAr at 1 pu) adds j0.19 to Y9,9.

把每条支路的四个元素累加到 14 × 14 矩阵中，就得到节点导纳矩阵，它把注入电流和节点电压联系起来：I = YbusV。对角元是与该节点相连的所有导纳之和（自导纳）；非对角元是两节点之间导纳的负值（互导纳），两节点不相连时为零。节点 9 的并联电容器（1 pu 电压下 19 MVAr）在 Y9,9 上加 j0.19。

Ybus of the current case当前算例的 Ybus

Each square is one entry Yik = Gik + jBik. Hover for its value. Empty squares are exact zeros: buses that share no branch.

每个方块是一个元素 Yik = Gik + jBik，悬停可查看数值。空白方块是零元素：两节点之间没有支路。

14 buses and 20 branches give 14 + 2 × 20 = 54 nonzeros out of 196 entries. Real grids with tens of thousands of buses are more than 99.9% zeros, which is why production solvers rely on sparse matrix techniques.

14 个节点、20 条支路，共 14 + 2 × 20 = 54 个非零元（总数 196）。实际电网有数万个节点，导纳矩阵 99.9% 以上是零，所以工程软件都采用稀疏矩阵技术。

Take a line out in the Lab (section 07) and watch its two off-diagonal entries disappear here.

在第 07 节的实验台断开一条线路，就能看到这里对应的两个非对角元消失。

04

The power flow equations潮流方程

The complex power injected at bus i is the voltage times the conjugate of the injected current. Substituting I = YbusV, writing Yik = Gik + jBik and θik = θi − θk, and separating real and imaginary parts gives two real equations per bus:

节点 i 的注入复功率等于电压乘以注入电流的共轭。代入 I = YbusV，记 Yik = Gik + jBik、θik = θi − θk，再分开实部和虚部，每个节点得到两个实数方程（极坐标形式）：

\[ S_i = P_i + jQ_i = V_i\,I_i^{*} = V_i\sum_{k=1}^{n} Y_{ik}^{*}\,V_k^{*} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} P_i &= |V_i|\sum_{k=1}^{n}|V_k|\left(G_{ik}\cos\theta_{ik}+B_{ik}\sin\theta_{ik}\right)\\ Q_i &= |V_i|\sum_{k=1}^{n}|V_k|\left(G_{ik}\sin\theta_{ik}-B_{ik}\cos\theta_{ik}\right)\end{aligned} \]

Both are nonlinear: voltage magnitudes multiply each other and the angles sit inside sines and cosines. There is no closed-form solution, so every method guesses a solution and corrects it. The quantity being driven to zero is the mismatch between what is scheduled at a bus and what the current guess implies:

这两个方程都是非线性的：电压幅值相互相乘，相角出现在正弦和余弦里。它们没有解析解，所有方法都是先猜一个解再不断修正。被迭代逼近零的量是功率不平衡量，即节点的给定功率与当前电压估计值算出的功率之差：

\[ \Delta P_i = \underbrace{P_{Gi}-P_{Di}}_{P_i^{\text{sch}}} - P_i(\theta,|V|),\qquad \Delta Q_i = \underbrace{Q_{Gi}-Q_{Di}}_{Q_i^{\text{sch}}} - Q_i(\theta,|V|) \]

Check it yourself. At a flat start (|V| = 1, θ = 0) the computed injections are almost zero, because each row of Ybus sums to nearly zero. So the first mismatch is essentially the scheduled injection itself. Bus 14 sits between two PQ buses, so there it is exactly −0.149 pu, its 14.9 MW load. At bus 3 it is −0.922 pu rather than −0.942, because the PV setpoints around it are not exactly 1. Find both at iteration 0 in the explorer below.

自己验证一下。平启动时（|V| = 1，θ = 0），算出的注入功率几乎为零，因为 Ybus 每一行的元素之和接近零。所以第一次的不平衡量基本就是给定注入本身。节点 14 两侧都是 PQ 节点，它的 ΔP 正好是 −0.149 pu，即 14.9 MW 的负荷；节点 3 则是 −0.922 pu 而不是 −0.942 pu，因为它周围 PV 节点的电压设定值不等于 1。可以在下面迭代过程的第 0 次迭代中找到这两个值。

05

Newton–Raphson, step by step牛顿–拉夫逊法：逐步求解

Collect the 22 unknowns into one vector and the 22 mismatches into another. A first-order Taylor expansion around the current guess tells us how far to move each unknown to cancel the mismatch. The matrix of partial derivatives is the Jacobian, split into four blocks:

把 22 个未知量排成一个向量，22 个不平衡量排成另一个向量。在当前估计点做一阶泰勒展开，就能算出每个未知量要修正多少才能消去不平衡量。偏导数组成的矩阵叫雅可比矩阵，分为四块：

\[ \begin{bmatrix}\Delta\mathbf P\\ \Delta\mathbf Q\end{bmatrix} = \underbrace{\begin{bmatrix}\mathbf H & \mathbf N\\ \mathbf M & \mathbf L\end{bmatrix}}_{\mathbf J}\begin{bmatrix}\Delta\theta\\ \Delta|\mathbf V|\end{bmatrix} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} H_{ik}&=\frac{\partial P_i}{\partial\theta_k}=|V_i||V_k|\left(G_{ik}\sin\theta_{ik}-B_{ik}\cos\theta_{ik}\right), & H_{ii}&=-Q_i-B_{ii}|V_i|^2\\ N_{ik}&=\frac{\partial P_i}{\partial|V_k|}=|V_i|\left(G_{ik}\cos\theta_{ik}+B_{ik}\sin\theta_{ik}\right), & N_{ii}&=\frac{P_i}{|V_i|}+G_{ii}|V_i|\\ M_{ik}&=\frac{\partial Q_i}{\partial\theta_k}=-|V_i||V_k|\left(G_{ik}\cos\theta_{ik}+B_{ik}\sin\theta_{ik}\right), & M_{ii}&=P_i-G_{ii}|V_i|^2\\ L_{ik}&=\frac{\partial Q_i}{\partial|V_k|}=|V_i|\left(G_{ik}\sin\theta_{ik}-B_{ik}\cos\theta_{ik}\right), & L_{ii}&=\frac{Q_i}{|V_i|}-B_{ii}|V_i| \end{aligned} \]

The algorithm算法步骤

  1. Flat start. Set θ = 0 everywhere, |V| = 1 at PQ buses, and the setpoint at PV and slack buses.
  2. Compute Pi, Qi from the current guess and form the mismatch vector.
  3. If the largest mismatch is below ε = 10−8 pu (1 W on a 100 MVA base), stop.
  4. Build J at the current guess and solve J Δx = [ΔP; ΔQ].
  5. Update θ ← θ + Δθ and |V| ← |V| + Δ|V|, then go back to step 2.
  6. After convergence, compute the slack bus P and Q, the PV-bus Q, branch flows and losses.
  1. 平启动。所有节点 θ = 0；PQ 节点 |V| = 1，PV 节点和平衡节点取电压设定值。
  2. 用当前电压估计值计算 Pi、Qi，形成不平衡量向量。
  3. 若最大不平衡量小于 ε = 10−8 pu（100 MVA 基准下即 1 W），停止迭代。
  4. 在当前点形成雅可比矩阵 J，求解修正方程 J Δx = [ΔP; ΔQ]。
  5. 更新 θ ← θ + Δθ，|V| ← |V| + Δ|V|，回到第 2 步。
  6. 收敛后，计算平衡节点的 P、Q，PV 节点的 Q，以及支路潮流和网损。

Close to the solution, Newton–Raphson converges quadratically: the number of correct digits roughly doubles every iteration. Step through it below and watch the exponent of the largest mismatch.

在解附近，牛顿–拉夫逊法具有二阶（平方）收敛性：每迭代一次，有效数字位数大约翻一倍。在下面逐次查看迭代过程，留意最大不平衡量的指数如何变化。

The Jacobian has the same sparsity pattern as Ybus: an entry is nonzero only where two buses share a branch. Its values change every iteration, so each Newton step means a new factorization. That is the cost the next section's methods try to avoid.

雅可比矩阵与 Ybus 的稀疏结构相同：只有两节点之间有支路时，对应元素才非零。它的数值每次迭代都会变，所以每一步牛顿迭代都要重新分解矩阵。下一节的方法正是想要省去这部分计算量。

06

Gauss–Seidel and fast decoupled高斯–赛德尔法与 P-Q 分解法

Gauss–Seidel

Gauss–Seidel solves each bus equation for its own voltage, one bus at a time, using the newest values of its neighbours:

高斯–赛德尔法逐个节点求解自身的电压方程，每次都使用相邻节点的最新值：

\[ V_i \leftarrow \frac{1}{Y_{ii}}\left(\frac{P_i^{\text{sch}}-jQ_i^{\text{sch}}}{V_i^{*}}-\sum_{k\neq i}Y_{ik}V_k\right) \]

At a PV bus, Qi is first estimated from the current voltages, and |Vi| is reset to its setpoint after the update. A sweep is cheap, but convergence is only linear and gets slower as the network grows. Here PQ-bus updates use an acceleration factor α = 1.6.

在 PV 节点，先用当前电压估算 Qi，更新之后再把 |Vi| 拉回设定值。每轮迭代计算量很小，但只有线性收敛，网络越大收敛越慢。本页对 PQ 节点使用加速因子 α = 1.6。

Fast decoupled load flowP-Q 分解法（快速解耦潮流）

Transmission networks have two useful properties: reactance dominates resistance (x ≫ r), and angle differences across branches are small. Then the N and M blocks are small, real power is coupled mainly to angles and reactive power to magnitudes, and the remaining blocks are nearly constant:

输电网有两个有用的特点：电抗远大于电阻（x ≫ r），支路两端相角差很小。于是 N、M 两块很小，有功主要与相角相关，无功主要与电压幅值相关，剩下的两块近似为常数：

\[ \mathbf B'\,\Delta\theta = \frac{\Delta\mathbf P}{|\mathbf V|},\qquad \mathbf B''\,\Delta|\mathbf V| = \frac{\Delta\mathbf Q}{|\mathbf V|},\qquad B'_{ik}=-\frac{1}{x_{ik}},\quad \mathbf B''=-\operatorname{Im}\mathbf Y_{\text{bus}} \]

This page uses the XB scheme: B′ is built from branch reactances only (no resistance, charging or taps), and B″ is the negative of the imaginary part of Ybus on the PQ buses. Both matrices are factored once and reused, so an iteration costs two cheap solves. The approximation only changes the path to the answer. The mismatches are always computed with the full equations, so the converged solution is identical to Newton–Raphson's.

本页采用 XB 型方案：B′ 只用支路电抗形成（忽略电阻、充电电纳和变比），B″ 取 PQ 节点对应的 Ybus 虚部的负值。两个矩阵只需分解一次就可反复使用，每次迭代只要两次很快的回代。近似只改变了迭代路径，不平衡量始终用完整方程计算，所以收敛结果与牛顿法完全相同。

Iteration counts alone are misleading. A Gauss–Seidel sweep touches each bus once. A fast decoupled iteration reuses two factorizations. A Newton iteration builds and factors a new 22 × 22 Jacobian. For large systems, Newton–Raphson with sparse factorization usually wins overall, and fast decoupled is popular wherever many similar cases must be solved quickly, such as contingency screening.

只比较迭代次数会产生误导。高斯–赛德尔法每轮只把每个节点算一遍；P-Q 分解法每次迭代复用两个已分解的矩阵；牛顿法每次都要重新形成并分解 22 × 22 的雅可比矩阵。对大系统来说，采用稀疏分解的牛顿法通常总体最快；需要快速求解大量相似算例时（例如预想事故扫描），P-Q 分解法很常用。
