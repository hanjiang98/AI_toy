02

What changes in the power flow problem潮流问题发生了什么变化

The network equations do not change. Ybus, the mismatch equations and Newton–Raphson are exactly the same as in a system with only conventional generators. A plant enters the problem as an extra injection at its bus:

网络方程没有变。Ybus、功率不平衡方程和牛顿–拉夫逊法，都与只有常规发电机的系统完全相同。电站只是在接入节点增加一项注入功率：

\[ P_i^{\text{sch}} = P_{Gi} + P_{Wi} + P_{Si} - P_{Di},\qquad Q_i^{\text{sch}} = Q_{Gi} + Q_{REi} - Q_{Di} \]

What is new is where those numbers come from, and what they do to the network:

新的问题在于这些数值从何而来，以及它们会对电网产生什么影响：

  • Power depends on the weather. PW and PS come from plant models driven by wind speed, irradiance and temperature. Sections 03 and 04 cover them.
  • Reactive power depends on the converter. The control mode decides whether the bus is PQ or PV, and the converter rating limits Q. Section 05 covers this.
  • Flows can reverse. Lines designed to carry power towards a load area may now carry it away, and weak buses may see voltage rise or overloads. Section 06 covers this.
  • The slack bus absorbs every change. In this model, any change in renewable output lands on the slack generator at bus 1. A real system shares it among many generators through dispatch and frequency control.
  • 有功取决于天气。PW、PS 由风速、辐照度和温度驱动的电站模型算出，见第 03、04 节。
  • 无功取决于变流器。控制方式决定节点是 PQ 还是 PV 类型，变流器容量限制 Q，见第 05 节。
  • 潮流可能反向。原本向负荷区送电的线路可能变成反向送电，薄弱节点可能出现电压升高或线路过载，见第 06 节。
  • 平衡节点承担所有变化。在本模型中，新能源出力的任何变化都由节点 1 的平衡机承担；实际系统则通过调度和频率控制，由多台机组分担。

A power flow is a snapshot. It finds one steady state for one set of inputs. It says nothing about stability, fault ride-through or fast weather changes. To study variability with it, you run many snapshots, as section 08 does.

潮流计算只是一张“快照”。它针对一组输入求一个稳态解，不能回答稳定性、故障穿越或天气快速变化的问题。要用它研究波动性，就得连续计算很多张快照，第 08 节就是这样做的。

03

Wind farm: from wind speed to megawatts风电场：从风速到兆瓦

Wind power grows with the cube of wind speed, so a small change in wind makes a large change in output. A turbine produces nothing below the cut-in speed vci, follows a cubic curve up to the rated speed vr, holds rated power by pitching its blades, and shuts down above the cut-out speed vco to protect itself.

风功率与风速的三次方成正比，因此风速的小变化会带来出力的大变化。风机在切入风速 vci 以下不发电，到额定风速 vr 之间近似沿三次曲线上升，之后靠变桨维持额定功率，超过切出风速 vco 时停机保护。

\[ P_W(v)=N P_t\,\eta_w \times \begin{cases} 0, & v\lt v_{ci}\ \text{or}\ v\ge v_{co}\\[3pt] \dfrac{v^3-v_{ci}^3}{v_r^3-v_{ci}^3}, & v_{ci}\le v\lt v_r\\[8pt] 1, & v_r\le v\lt v_{co} \end{cases} \]

Here N is the number of turbines, Pt is the rating of each, and ηw covers wake losses and turbines out of service. The farm is treated as one equivalent machine at its bus, and the same wind speed is applied to every turbine. Real power curves come from the manufacturer; the cubic is a common textbook approximation.

式中 N 为风机台数，Pt 为单机容量，ηw 计及尾流损失和机组停运。整个风电场等值为接在节点上的一台机组，所有风机使用同一风速。实际功率曲线由厂家提供，三次曲线是教材中常用的近似。

04

Solar plant: irradiance, temperature and clipping光伏电站：辐照度、温度与限幅

DC power is nearly proportional to irradiance G, and it falls as the cells heat up. A simple estimate of cell temperature uses the panel's nominal operating cell temperature (NOCT):

直流功率基本与辐照度 G 成正比，并随电池温度升高而下降。电池温度可以用组件的标称工作温度（NOCT）简单估算：

\[ T_c = T_a + \frac{\text{NOCT}-20}{800}\,G,\qquad P_{dc}=P_{\text{STC}}\,\frac{G}{1000}\left[1+\gamma\,(T_c-25)\right] \]

The inverter then limits what reaches the grid. Plants usually have more DC panels than inverter capacity, because panels rarely reach their nameplate power. The ratio is the DC/AC ratio, and the energy lost at the limit is called clipping:

逆变器进一步限制送入电网的功率。电站的直流组件容量通常大于逆变器容量，因为组件很少达到铭牌功率，两者之比称为容配比（DC/AC），超过逆变器容量被削掉的部分称为限幅损失：

\[ P_S=\min\left(\eta_s\,P_{dc},\ S_{\text{inv}}\right),\qquad S_{\text{inv}}=\frac{P_{\text{STC}}}{\text{DC/AC}} \]
05

Reactive control decides the bus type无功控制决定节点类型

Modern wind turbines and solar inverters connect through power electronics, so their reactive power is set by the controller. The limit is the converter's apparent power rating S. Active power takes its share first, and reactive power gets what is left:

现代风机和光伏逆变器都通过电力电子变流器并网，无功由控制器设定，其限制来自变流器的视在功率容量 S。有功优先，无功只能使用剩余容量：

\[ |Q_{RE}| \le Q_{\text{cap}}=\sqrt{S^2-P^2} \]

The control mode decides how the bus appears in the power flow:

控制方式决定该节点在潮流计算中的形式：

Mode控制方式Bus type节点类型Specified给定Solved待求In the Newton iteration在牛顿迭代中
Constant power factor恒功率因数PQP, Q = P tan φ|V|, θWorks like a negative load.相当于负的负荷。
Voltage control恒电压PVP, |V|Q, θBecomes PQ at Q = ±Qcap.达到 Q = ±Qcap 时转为 PQ 节点。
Q(V)PQP, Q(|V|)|V|, θAdds a term to the Jacobian diagonal.在雅可比矩阵对角元上增加一项。

Q(V) control, also called volt-var droop, is the most interesting case for the solver. The scheduled reactive power now depends on the unknown voltage:

Q(V) 控制（电压–无功下垂）对求解器来说最有意思：给定的无功现在取决于未知的电压：

\[ Q_{RE}(|V|)=S\,\frac{V_{\text{ref}}-|V|}{D},\quad\text{limited to}\ \pm Q_{\text{cap}} \]

Because the mismatch is now ΔQ = Qsch(|V|) − Q(θ, |V|), the derivative of the schedule enters the Jacobian. Off the limits, the diagonal of block L gains a term S/D (in per unit), and Newton–Raphson keeps its fast convergence. At a limit, the term disappears and Q is constant.

由于不平衡量变成 ΔQ = Qsch(|V|) − Q(θ, |V|)，给定值的导数也要进入雅可比矩阵。未达到限值时，L 块对角元增加一项 S/D（标幺值），牛顿法仍然保持快速收敛；达到限值时这一项消失，Q 为常数。

\[ L_{ii}=\frac{Q_i}{|V_i|}-B_{ii}|V_i|+\frac{S}{D} \]
06

What the grid feels: voltage, flows and losses电网的响应：电压、潮流与网损

Consider a plant injecting P + jQ at the end of a line with impedance R + jX. For small angle differences, the voltage at the plant rises by approximately:

设电站经阻抗为 R + jX 的线路注入功率 P + jQ。相角差较小时，电站处的电压升高约为：

\[ \Delta V \approx \frac{R\,P + X\,Q}{|V|} \]
  • Voltage rise. Active power raises voltage through R, and reactive power acts through X. Where X > R, as on most of this network, absorbing a modest Q can cancel much of the rise caused by P.
  • Reverse flow. When a plant's output exceeds the local load, the surplus flows back through lines that normally feed that area. Those lines may be too small for it, even if their voltages look fine.
  • Losses follow a U-shape. Generation close to load shortens the path power travels, so losses fall at first. Past a certain share the surplus has to travel back across the network, and losses rise again.
  • 电压升高。有功通过 R 抬升电压，无功通过 X 起作用。在 X > R 的线路上（本网络大多如此），吸收不多的 Q 就能抵消 P 带来的大部分电压升高。
  • 潮流反向。电站出力超过当地负荷时，多余功率经原来向该地区供电的线路倒送。即使电压看起来正常，这些线路也可能容量不足。
  • 网损呈 U 形。电源靠近负荷时，功率传输路径缩短，网损先下降；比例超过一定程度后，多余功率要反向穿过电网，网损又会上升。

同样一座电站，接在不同节点上的影响差别很大。下表在当前实验台算例的基础上，把一座指定容量、单位功率因数的新电站依次接到每个节点，每次都求解一遍潮流。请对比网损变化和限值检查：网损最低的节点不一定可用，电压看起来正常的节点也可能使小容量线路过载。

08

Twenty-four snapshots of one day一天的 24 张快照

Running one power flow per hour is the simplest way to study variability. This is called a quasi-static time series. The chart uses the plants from the Lab with the hourly weather and load of the selected day. These profiles are illustrative values written for this page, not measured data.

每小时计算一次潮流，是研究波动性最简单的方法，称为准静态时序仿真。图中使用实验台里的电站，以及所选典型日的逐时天气和负荷。这些曲线是为本页编写的示意数据，不是实测数据。

Watch the gap between load and conventional generation. Solar opens it around midday and leaves a steep evening ramp, which is the "duck curve" shape. Wind output often follows a different pattern, which can make the combined output less variable.

留意负荷与常规机组出力之间的差距。光伏在中午把差距拉大，傍晚留下一段陡峭的爬坡，也就是“鸭子曲线”。风电出力通常有不同的日变化规律，两者组合后总出力的波动可能更小。

What this model leaves out. Each plant is one equivalent machine connected directly to its bus, without a collector network or step-up transformer. All plants share one weather input, apart from a site factor. The page covers steady-state balanced operation only: no frequency response, stability, fault ride-through, harmonics or forecast uncertainty. It is for teaching, not for planning studies.

本模型未考虑的内容。每座电站等值为一台直接接在节点上的机组，没有集电网络和升压变压器；除场址系数外，所有电站使用同一组天气输入。本页只涉及三相平衡稳态运行，不考虑频率响应、稳定性、故障穿越、谐波和预测不确定性。仅用于教学，不能用于规划研究。
